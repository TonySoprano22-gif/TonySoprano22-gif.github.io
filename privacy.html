Privacy Policy

Jarvis — last updated 16 August 2026

Summary

Jarvis runs entirely on the operator's own computer. It has no server, no database, and no other users. It does not collect personal information about anyone, and there is nothing to sell, share, or hand over.

What is stored, and where

Everything Jarvis stores is stored locally on the computer it runs on:

  • Platform credentials. Access and refresh tokens for the operator's own accounts, encrypted using the operating system's credential store. They are never transmitted anywhere except to the platform that issued them.
  • Generated content. Scripts, audio, images, and finished video files.
  • Publishing records. Which videos were produced and where they were sent.

What is sent to third parties

Producing and uploading a video requires contacting a small number of services. Each receives only what it needs to do its part:

  • TikTok and YouTube — the finished video file and its title, uploaded to the operator's own account.
  • Text-to-speech, image search, and language model providers — the script text or search terms needed to generate narration and select imagery.

No personal information about any viewer, follower, or other person is sent to any of them.

What is not done

  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking, or advertising identifiers.
  • No reading of another user's TikTok data. Jarvis requests only the ability to upload to the connected account and to read that account's basic information.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone, for any purpose.

Retention and deletion

Because all data is local, the operator controls it entirely. Disconnecting an account removes its stored tokens. Deleting the application's data folder removes everything else.
