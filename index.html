Jarvis

A desktop app for producing and publishing short documentary videos.

Jarvis researches real criminal cases from public sources, writes and narrates a short script, and assembles a vertical video from licensed stock imagery. Finished videos are uploaded to the account owner's own social media accounts.

It runs on the operator's own computer. It is not a service offered to the public: there are no user accounts, and it uploads only to the account of the person running it.
